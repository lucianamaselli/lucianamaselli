# Hi, I’m Luciana Maselli

I’m an applied researcher drawn to public problems I keep coming back to. One of them is access to sanitation in Brazil’s favelas. In my research, I look at what existing studies tell us about sanitation in these communities and whose experiences are still missing from the evidence.

I work with unstructured data, from historical documents and newspaper archives to open-ended survey responses. My dream is to bring different kinds of data together to help solve public problems. I love social experiments, especially field experiments that let us test ideas and learn how they play out in people’s lives.

## What I work on

- **Sanitation in Brazil’s favelas.** I review and map evidence on sanitation, health, and living conditions, looking at which outcomes researchers measure and where gaps remain.
- **Work and inequality.** I support a field experiment on access to gig work and exposure to inequality, working on survey design, data quality, and workflows for analyzing qualitative responses.
- **Historical demography.** I turn historical Italian census tables into structured datasets and use text analysis to study how U.S. newspapers discussed fertility and population control.
- **Responsible AI in public institutions.** I work on AI governance, tool evaluation, and training for public-sector teams and international organizations.

I’ve decided to start sharing my projects here. I’ll add them over time, with the questions behind them, the methods I used, and code and data where I can share them. I work with **R, Python, and Stata** and welcome collaborations in applied social science, public policy, and responsible AI.
